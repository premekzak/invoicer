# UI

## Principles (from the owner)

- **Always show the next step.** The Home screen's table of things to check (under the greeting, next to the "Nová faktura" tile; captioned "Co dál" cards until 2026-10-17) comes from the data: create an invoice, N overdue invoices, missing profile fields, review customers. New screens should do the same.
- **Smooth and fast:**
  - keyboard-first editors: the customer search works with ↑ ↓ Enter; Enter in a line description adds the next line, which takes focus
  - no page reloads
  - short rise animations (`animate-rise`)
  - hover actions on list rows
- **Never lose work:** autosave with a visible status; Finish and Issue wait for the save to finish; leaving the editor through "Upravit odběratele" (Edit customer) saves a draft first.
- **Explain in plain words.** The owner isn't a developer. Error and confirmation text says what happened and what to do.

## Design

Invojka is built on the design kit in `src/design/`, documented in **[design/](design/README.md)**: colour roles and state rules ([tokens](design/tokens.md)), the scheme contract and the eleven schemes ([schemes](design/schemes.md)), the type scale ([typography](design/typography.md)), and layout and component patterns ([components](design/components.md)). Those pages are generic; keep them free of invoices and Czech. This section records only what Invojka chooses on top of them. The components are in `src/ui/primitives.tsx` (Button, Input, Select, Textarea, InputAffix, Labeled, Field, Checkbox, ErrorSummary, Card, Choice, SettingRow) and `src/ui/format.tsx` (StatusBadge).

### Faces

- **Inter** for everything (`font-sans`), with `ss01` (open digits); don't turn on `cv11`. Inter 400 and 600 are the TTFs shared with the PDF; 500 is a UI-only woff2 subset. All in `src/pdf/fonts.ts` (`loadUiFonts`).
- **Roboto 700** for page and section titles (`font-display`; the owner's trial, 2026-10-17, after Newsreader and Bricolage Grotesque). A plain grotesque like Inter, so headlines stand out by size and weight.
- **Roboto 500** for the header tabs only (`font-nav`, the owner's choice "R3", 2026-10-17). Don't use Roboto elsewhere without asking.
- **The wordmark is lowercase, "invojka"** (the owner, 2026-10-19; `WORDMARK` in `src/ui/Logo.tsx`), in the display face at 18 px, in the header, the welcome screen and the footer. Running text, the window title and the PDF keep "Invojka".

### Warm paper, the default scheme

Its values are in `src/design/schemes/warm.css` and its summary in [design/schemes.md](design/schemes.md#the-schemes). The owner's choices and exceptions, recorded so nobody "fixes" them:
- **A tinted header and a cream page.** The peach band is a large tinted area, and the page is faintly cream rather than white (the colour rules would keep both muted). Picked as "F4" and "O1" on 2026-10-17.
- **Burnt-orange accent** `#cf4a09` with white text (2026-10-18, replacing the blue). It is 3.95:1 on the page, so text links and focus use the darker `link` `#a94e00`.
- **The owner's orange `#fa8d4e`** is only the logo's marks (2.3:1 on white, never text or anything clickable).
- **Ochre for warnings, crimson for errors** (2026-10-17). The owner finds red aggressive, so overdue and missing rates are ochre, always with a word; errors and delete stay crimson because they are rare.
- **Charts in one orange and its shades** (the owner: "at most two colours in charts, then only shades"): `series-1` orange, `series-2` pale peach, the tax donut dark, bar and light orange.
- **Flat everywhere except the footer** (espresso, a slight gradient, the same in both modes). Gradients on buttons, the header and the tile were tried on 2026-10-17 and found too much.

### Schemes in the app

Settings → Aplikace → Barevné schéma (`settings.ui.scheme`); light and dark come from Vzhled, so every scheme has both. `src/App.tsx` sets `<html data-scheme>` (none for `warm`). The list is `SCHEMES` in `src/domain/schema.ts`; a saved id that is no longer in it falls back to Warm paper (`.catch('warm')`), so removing a scheme can't stop a folder from loading. `src/domain/schemes.test.ts` checks that every id has a file in the kit, an import in `src/index.css` and a name in both languages. The owner's briefs and reference images are in `schemes/<name>/` (only `zadani.md` is committed).

### Where Invojka uses the kit's patterns

- **Header:** logo and wordmark (links to Home), the five pages as tabs, the business switcher on the right with the save tick before it. No sidebar (2026-10-17). "Nová faktura" is not in the header.
- **Save tick:** a `Check` in `header-fg`; its tooltip says "Vše uloženo v 18:05", the folder name and that the browser hides the full path. Saving is a small spinner in `header-muted`; an error or a conflict is a solid crimson chip with words. Demo mode looks the same.
- **Business switcher:** the business name with a `ChevronsUpDown` icon opens a menu aligned right: each business with its folder name under it (only when it differs), a `Check` in `accent` on the current one, a `rule` divider, then "Přidat další firmu" in `accent` with a hint.
- **Footer:** the logo (`.on-inverse`), the wordmark and the tagline on the left; "Bez cloudu a bez účtu. Funguje i offline." and a link to invojka.cz on the right.
- **Feature block:** "Nová faktura" on Home (the owner's choice "K2", 2026-10-17), 17.5 rem wide, a 44 px plus and a one-line hint. Next to it at equal height, a white card listing what needs checking: its first line says how many (ochre alert icon), then one striped row per problem with a dot, a title, a detail and a small link button; overdue invoices are ochre, the rest neutral. With nothing to check it is one line: a green check, "Vše v pořádku".
- **Home greeting** always ends with a full stop, or a question mark for a question (`src/i18n/greetings.test.ts`). Picked by time of day from `home.greetings.<slot>` (`dawn` 5–9, `morning` 9–12, `noon` 12–14, `afternoon` 14–18, `evening` 18–22, `night`), one per day and slot (`pickDaily`). Never the owner's name: Czech would need the vocative ("Dobrý den, Jana" was wrong). The jokes ("pane Továrníku") were the owner's idea.
- **Home summary:** one or two sentences in the lead size, `max-w-prose`, numbers semibold in `fg`, the overdue part in `warning`, amounts in whole units (`f.moneyWhole`). It covers invoices and revenue this year, the change against the same point last year (`yearTrend`), what's waiting and how much is overdue; its variants: no invoices yet, everything paid, nothing overdue, some overdue, all overdue.
- **VAT registration limit** card (non-payers and identified persons): this year's turnover against 2,000,000 Kč with the percentage, a thin bar (`accent`, `warning` from 80 %), and one muted line on the two limits, the foreign invoices left out and that it's an estimate. From 80 % a task row appears too. Rules in [invoicing-rules.md](invoicing-rules.md#vat-registration-limit).
- **Tax donut** (`features/home/TaxEstimate.tsx`, the owner's choice 2026-10-18): in the right half of the tax card, 280 px with a 34 px ring; tax, social and health in `cat-1` … `cat-3`, the rest of the income as the track with "Zbyde z příjmů", the amount and the share in the middle (or "Odvody převyšují příjmy"). Scaled to the income, or to the payments when they are larger.
- **Revenue charts** (`ui/BarChart.tsx`, `features/home/RevenueCharts.tsx`): only revenue (issued plus paid) in CZK; foreign currencies in [exchange-rates.md](exchange-rates.md). Incomplete periods say "zatím, k {date}".
- **Invoice list:** status labels right-aligned, so they end at the same edge as the amount column (the owner, 2026-10-17); "Zaplaceno" as an icon button before the status. On the customer list "+ Nová faktura" is an `accent` link after the last-invoice date, on hover or focus.
- **Phones:** a closable notice (`ui/DesktopNotice.tsx`, once per visit) says Invojka is a desktop app and why (mobile browsers can't open a folder); Home's recent invoices drop the number and date. Checked at 390 px on every screen.
- **PDF:** neutral by default (warm ink greys, a near-black "to pay" box; the stamps are ink too). A user may pick one accent in Settings → Vzhled faktury (blue, teal, green, wine, orange; each at least 4.5:1 with white text); it colours only the title and the "to pay" box, so the PDF still prints well in black and white. All PDF colours are `PDF_COLORS` in `src/pdf/palette.ts`, which Settings reads for its swatches. The PDF doesn't follow the app's scheme.
